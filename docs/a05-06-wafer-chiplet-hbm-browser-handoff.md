## Four source-aligned stages and exact physics
| sceneId | Required evidence to pass plus correct answer key | Scientific/negative contract |
|---|---|---|
| `fab-pattern-to-die` | `ordered-fab,layers1,layers2,defect0,defect1`, WHY `repeat-and-test` | `wafer→photo→process(etch/deposit/implant)→metal→test→dice` **ordered** toy six-step FSM, `INVALID_STAGE` for premature dice/metal/test, `RESET` removes completed sequence proof. Masks/layer1/2 and defect marker are qualitative, not measured fab. m1 one photolithography step sufficient, m2 100% wafer die good, m3 process node naming equals exact physical transistor size all separately wrong. |
| `chiplet-yield-model` | `density0,density005,none,KGD,assembly1,assembly095`, WHY `conditional-kgd-package-yield` | Toy D=.005/mm² `mono Y=e^-0.5≈0.606531`, `one25mm² Y=e^-.125≈0.882497`, `four unscreened=one^4≈0.606531`, with assembly.95 full-good ≈0.576204. **Only after ideal KGD selection of four known-good dies from sufficiently many candidate die** is the *conditional package assembly pass* .95; still average `4/0.882497≈4.5326` fabricated candidates (and tests/stock/escapes not free). D0 and assembly1/.95 independent; NEVER claim dividing a die unconditionally improves complete product yield, or `4×small` instead of `small⁴`. Actual KGD imperfect. m1/m2/m3 distinct. |
| `hbm-tsv-interposer` | `board,stack,interposer-silicon,interposer-RDL,tsv`, WHY `tsv-internal-interposer-sideways` | Board GDDR traces separate from DRAM stack's **vertical TSV**. Both silicon and RDL-based package methods possible; UI 2.5D picture ≠ silicon 2.5D advanced packaging. Claim GPU and HBM are always one die, only silicon interposer, or UI renderer equals packaging are three wrong branches. |
| `package-power-heat` | `power20,power40,r02,r04`, WHY `p-times-r-thermal` | Toy constant ambient 25°C, steady-one-path heat `ΔT=P×Rθ`: 20W×.2K/W=4K →29°C; 40W×.4K/W=16K→41°C; cross pairs both8K. Do NOT assert actual GPU junction temperature. TSV/fan not zero heat or watt, Rθ not universal fixed real-physical constant. |
