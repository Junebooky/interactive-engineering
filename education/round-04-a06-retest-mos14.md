- `Cox′(5nm)=0.006906266494F/m²=0.6906266494µF/cm²`; `Cox′(10nm)=0.3453133247µF/cm²`;
- `VT(5nm)=0.784825363V`; `VT(10nm)=0.855333575V` when other inputs fixed, **not** M01 separate toy Vth0.7V. Not universal silicon product specs. **Arithmetic all PASS**.
- MIT OCW 6.012 Fall 2009 Lecture 9 [original PDF, slides 1,8,13,14](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-fall-2009/42c863e2e1e9744ce6b797646a30e463_MIT6_012F09_lec09.pdf) independently opened and visually examined 2026-10-17. p-Si `VGB<VFB` has hole accumulation, positive bias moves from depletion to inversion; `VFB` is **not** generally zero; lecture shows realistic zero-bias depletion. Threshold equation `VT=VFB+2φF+|Qdep|/Cox` agrees under 1D assumptions. Gate oxide electrostatics is distinct from intended DC gate conduction; two-terminal capacitor has **no terminal ID**. The source correctly distinguishes low/high frequency C–V and the source's ideal `VDS=0` zero net drain current vs bias-induced nonzero.
