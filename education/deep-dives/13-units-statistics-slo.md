## Physical/measurement model and exact source
**1. Bytes and binary prefixes.** NIST [U1] uses SI G=10^9 while IEC binary Gi=2^30. 1GB=1,000,000,000B; 1GiB=1,073,741,824B=1.073741824GB. B=byte and b=bit, 1B=8b; unit symbols case-sensitive. 1GB/s of ideal payload bandwidth would take 1s for exactly 1GB but 1.073741824s to send 1GiB; real headers, queues, contention and limited receiver can make it longer. No storage vendor capacity claims.
**2. DDR transfers vs PCIe signaling.** A teaching *64-bit data-only* DDR channel operating at **6400MT/s** yields 64bit×6.4×10^9 transfers/s÷8bit/byte =51.2×10^9 B/s =51.2 GB/s theoretical upper limit, NOT observed user payload nor first-read latency. PCI-SIG [U2] says PCIe6 x16 **256GB/s bidirectional aggregate**; ideal symmetric one way≈128GB/s; PAM4 already accounted in the advertised 64GT/s and must not be multiplied again ×2. PCIe7 published x16 512GB/s both directions in 29 lesson; never infer exact app payload from GT/s without negotiated lanes, protocol overhead or encoding.
**3. Poisson/exp/log.** Toy independent homogeneous random defects of density D=.005 defects/mm² over area A=100mm²: mean λ=D A=.5, P(0 defects)=e^(−λ)=0.6065306597, P(≥1)=0.3934693403, and inverse D=−ln(P0)/A=.005/mm². Splitting 100 into 4×25mm² yields per-small-die e^(−.125)=.8824969026 and **4 randomly chosen unscreened dies simultaneously good** (.8824969026)^4=.6065306597, without a magical package yield gain. KGD screened selection and assembly yield are **separate conditional denominators**, covered in Lesson06.
**4. Quantile from raw observations vs histogram estimate.** Label estimator **nearest-rank exact within raw sample**: sort n observations, r=ceil(q n), return 1-indexed sample r, 0<q≤1. For n100 A=95 at1ms and5 at20ms, p99 rank99=20ms, mean(95×1+5×20)/100=1.95ms; B=99 at1ms+1 at20ms yields p99 rank99=1ms, max20ms, mean1.19ms. Nearest-rank is one explicit convention—statistics packages may choose interpolation, not all percentiles definitions identical. For n5 raw 10,20,30,40,90ms, nearest-rank p90 rankceil4.5=5⇒90ms. Under *classic Prometheus* coarse buckets ≤50ms count4 and ≤100ms count5 (plus +Inf total5), `histogram_quantile(0.9, ...)` linearly interpolates within (50,100] because target count4.5⇒approx75ms, not actual raw rank5=90ms. Based on official [U3] and [U4] Prometheus docs. A toy n5 exaggerates sampling/bucket uncertainty; do NOT assert histogram point estimate is exact observed p90.
**5. SLI, SLO, availability and errors.** Following Google SRE [U5], define who counts as eligible (requests, service, region, period) and response-good criteria before ratio. Toy 1,000 eligible requests in fixed observation window, 99.9% successful-request SLO ⇒ ≥999 good, maximum1 bad (error budget1). 990 good/10 bad ⇒ observed good ratio 99.0%, ten bad use **10×** the allowed 1, exceed by9. Example that an SLO is a measurement/goal contract, **not** p99 or hardware uptime probability; not production SLA, penalties, RTO or failure independence. If exclusion/filter or rolling window changes, denominator changes; PUE energy ratio is unrelated.
