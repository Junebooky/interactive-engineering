## Suggested core spine (not an A07 takeover of A06 priorities)
`B00-A (electric foundations) → B00-B (silicon, carriers, PN) → B00-C (MOS capacitor, n/p) → 01 MOSFET/CMOS → 05 SRAM → 02 DRAM → 07 DDR/ECC → 03 GPU & GDDR/HBM → 06 manufacturing/chiplet/packaging → 04 NAND/eSSD → 09 SSD reliability → 08 fabric → 10 AI/DC`; CPU/GPU compute, PCIe and power/cooling units are **prerequisite inserts** at relevant bridges, not assumed learnt merely because they appear in the Atlas index. A06 should independently validate and finalize a **branching DAG**, not treat this suggested linear reading order as an enforced dependency chain.
