### Terminology bounds
- Under **ideal DC source** with battery terminal labels 5V and no current path, at steady-state OPEN the **DC conduction current in the one modeled branch is 0**, but source terminal potential difference can be 5V. The voltage **across the resistor** must not be mislabeled 5V when switch OPEN; resistive DC drop is0 for zero current under the ideal assumptions. With switch CLOSED, ideal5V across1kΩ ⇒ **I=5mA**, ignoring wire, internal source impedance/leakage/inductive and transient behavior. Real electronics have instrumentation/leakage nuances; these are not asserted measured. **Do not generalize “every circuit node 5V”**.
- Battery label 5V and resistor 1kΩ are **given fixture parameters** that can safely appear before prediction (also in SVG alt), not claims that actual open-source voltage persisted when tested. Avoid displaying “0mA”, “5mA”, “전류 흐르지 않음”, “전류만 멈춤”, “배터리5V는 남음/유지” as teacher conclusion in S00 or PRE.
- Keep only **one prediction question** and no physical switch in Problem before prediction. Dock appears after prediction, and **actual learner SEND/TOGGLE/OBSERVE events**, not defaults or mere prediction, create `history.open/closed` evidence. The fixed source label 5V does NOT mint a measurement event.
- The cognitive task “electron motion opposite to conventional current” must be discovered by observation, not stated as achieved answer in Step0; pre concept may teach electrons carry negative charge, but not the full assessed flow-direction result.
